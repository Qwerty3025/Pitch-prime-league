2026 Pitch Prime League

The Pitch Prime League (PPL) is Nigeria's most exciting digital-first 6-a-side indoor football tournament. Hosted in world-class stadiums in Lagos and Abuja, PPL brings together cultural icons, content creators, and elite young footballers.

Integrating state-of-the-art interactive graphics, live streams, and high-impact entertainment events, PPL connects football, lifestyle, and music under one glowing roof. Join us for a new season of raw rivalry, dynamic gameplay cards, and pure excitement.
